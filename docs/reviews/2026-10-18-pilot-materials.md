## Decision

Revision 1 is unsuitable for the intended unfamiliar-transfer assessment without revision. Its graph/print checks passed, but those checks did not detect repeated questions and response cues. Preserve revision 1 for audit; use [revision 2](../materials/pilot-v2/README.md) as the next review candidate. Both remain draft. Do not recruit or report transfer/retention gains from these materials until content, equivalence, paper/device and coordination gates are resolved.

The first three findings are identifiable material overlap, not observed learner memorization. The last two are potential confounds, not measured effects. Matching a bearing or shortest-path count cannot establish equal difficulty: alternate-route counts, visual clutter, closure salience, endpoint degree and reading burden still differ.

## What the desk checks establish

All forty candidate questions have authored answers tied to their map. Synthetic topology explicitly permits both directions; closures block both directed edges. Multiple example routes are accepted by the existing rubric oracle; examples are not an answer whitelist. Published packs remain draft, so they do not generate approved scores. Required clues and label sizes are retained; no real travel permission is inferred.

Per-item technical checks do not establish instructional clarity, response tolerance appropriateness, independent review, actual paper measurement or learning transfer. The [revision-2 checklist](../materials/pilot-v2/review-checklist.md) records all forty item identities and leaves independent sign-off pending.

## Required outside walkthrough before approval

1. Inspect each question and key with its labeled/partial map, both Letter layouts and intended learner device. Check start/end reference correspondence, closure visibility, ambiguity and accepted alternatives. Blank views are not valid for these questions.
2. Check that paper and screen responses measure the same skill. Record endpoint marks consistently (use the center of a cross) and use calibrated scale to apply the existing 8 m tolerance; do not invent a new paper threshold. Assess explanations manually with the existing 0/1/2/null rubric. Keep first responses distinct from hints/retries.
3. Compare all five sets for visual/reading burden and shared content, especially B/C training equivalence and A/D/E assessment difficulty. Document whether the remaining density/overlap differences require revision. Do not set ConditionsComparable from structural checks alone.
4. Record actual reviewer role/date, item findings, revisions and evidence. IndependentContent and ConditionsComparable stay null until those reviews occur. If approval requires revision, publish a new pack revision and update exact capture hashes together; retain earlier references.
5. Coordinate real permissions, familiarity, exposure time and 5–9-day follow-up under the existing protocol. No participation is supplied by a software test or a merge.

Until these gates are resolved, the release remains material preparation. Further terrain/app feature expansion stays paused. The separate Redmond source-backed packet still needs its own source/content/access review.
